## 3. Ad skipping and Podvo Plus

Podvo finds the adverts in an episode so it can skip them. Everything else it works out
about an episode, such as chapters, captions, the transcript and who is on it, is free.

**Free episodes.** Each week, Monday to Sunday in your time, Podvo skips the adverts in
some episodes for free: currently three, and the app shows how many you have left. One is
used when you start playing an episode that has adverts. An episode without adverts doesn't
use one. A used episode stays ad-free for seven days, so you can finish it.

**Podvo Plus** skips the adverts in every episode. It is a monthly subscription sold through
Google Play, at the price shown in the app and in Google Play before you buy. Google Play
takes the payment and renews Plus each month until you cancel.

- **Free trial:** new subscribers may get one. Cancel before it ends and you won't be charged.
- **Cancelling:** any time, in Google Play or from Podvo under **Settings > Ad skipping >
  Manage subscription**. Plus keeps working until the end of the month you've paid for.
- **Refunds:** Google Play handles them, under its refund policy.
- **Devices:** Plus works on up to five devices signed in to the Google account you bought
  it with.
- **Price changes:** Google Play tells subscribers before a new price applies, and you can
  cancel first.
- **If Plus stops:** we'll stop renewing it and say so in the app.

**Founding listeners and codes.** If you used Podvo before Plus began, ad skipping stays
free for you on that phone once you update. If you then reinstall or change phone, email us
and we'll send you a code. We may also give out Podvo codes that make ad skipping free for
a time or for good. Codes are gifts: they can't be sold or exchanged for money.

Ad skipping isn't perfect: it can miss an advert or skip a few seconds of the programme.
