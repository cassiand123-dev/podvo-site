Topics

Go straight to what you're into.

Podvo sorts a channel's whole back catalogue into topics, grouped under headings like Entertainment. Pick Film and Television and its own topics open beneath it, down to Documentary Film. The Joe Rogan Experience has 115 of them across 2,782 episodes.

Hosts and guests

Who's on, at a glance.

Every episode shows who hosts it and who's visiting, with their photo. Tap a guest to see who they are and every show they've been on: Gary Sinise turns up on 8 channels.

Clips

Hear the best bits first.

Swipe through moments from your shows, with each word lighting up as it's said. Like one? Keep listening from right there. Or snip it, and keep it to share as a short video.

New from your channels

Everything new, in the order it came out.

Home keeps a timeline of what your channels have just put out: today, yesterday, two days ago. Play one straight from the row, without leaving the page.

Chapters

Jump to the bit you came for.

Long episodes come split into chapters, each with a line on what it covers. Tap one and you're there.

Read along

The words, as they're said.

Swipe up on the player and the transcript follows along, each line lighting up as it's spoken. Under it sit the chapters, what the episode is about, and who's on it. Drag the bar and the words show where you'll land.

Watch, or just listen

The video keeps going while you browse.

When a show is filmed, watch it in the player. Leave, and it shrinks into the corner and carries on. Switch to audio and it picks up where the picture left off.

Up Next

A queue that fills itself.

Pick the channels you never miss, and their new episodes join the bottom of your queue. Play a whole channel in order, or put an episode next.

A channel's page takes its colours from the cover. Choose how they show: polka dots, a soft fade, two glows or one long fade down the page.

And the rest

All the little things.

  • DownloadsSave episodes for the plane. They finish in the background.
  • Cast to your TVSend what's playing to a Chromecast or smart TV.
  • Android AutoYour queue and shows on the car's screen.
  • Listen LaterPark an episode for later. It leaves once you've heard it.
  • Smart playlistsBuild a playlist from rules, by channel or subject.
  • Voice boostLifts quiet voices without making the loud ones louder.
  • Trim silenceCuts the pauses, so a long episode takes less time.
  • Share linksSend a channel or an episode. It opens in Podvo, or points to the Play Store.
