# Podvo

**A simple podcast player for Android.**

Podvo plays your podcasts, skips the adverts in them, and keeps your queue and listening
in order. There's no account to make, and your library stays on your phone.

* Follow channels and get new episodes automatically
* Skip adverts, intros and sponsor reads
* Up Next queue, playlists and Listen Later
* Chapters, topics and guests for each episode
* Sleep timer, Android Auto and casting to your TV

**[Get Podvo on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.podplay.app)**
